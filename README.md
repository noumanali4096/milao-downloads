- **[Download Customer ARM64 — 22.65 MB, recommended for most phones](https://github.com/noumanali4096/milao-downloads/releases/download/milao-testing-20261113-build49/MILAO-Customer-Build49-ARM64.apk)**

Choose one Customer APK. Build 49 is a redesign of the whole app. You can now browse restaurants and menus before signing in, and are asked for an account the first time you add a dish. It adds one-tap adding from the menu, menu search, "Order again" under a restaurant's menu, "Frequently bought together" on a dish, and "Popular with your order" in the cart. Checkout shows the restaurant's payment account where a restaurant takes online transfers, and Raast is supported as a transfer method. A food-type row appears on the home screen once restaurants have been tagged. It retains reviews, Help & Support and the earlier order improvements.

## Restaurant

**[Open Restaurant staging](https://restaurant-staging.milao.dev)** — Reviews and Help & Support are now available alongside Orders and Dispatch. Authorized owners/managers can publish one review response; restaurant support tickets preserve tenant and finance-role restrictions. Orders remains the primary workspace for normal order handling. Where online transfers are enabled, a restaurant can now add a Raast account beside JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfer.
